# JA verified U.S. live channel catalog

Sources: iptv-org API, https://iptv-org.github.io/iptv/countries/us.m3u, all eighteen requested category playlists under https://iptv-org.github.io/iptv/categories/, and https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Free-TV/IPTV/master/playlist.m3u8. iptv-org metadata is CC0; Free-TV remains attributed to https://github.com/Free-TV/IPTV. Only U.S. channels are imported; category-playlist country eligibility is checked against iptv-org's channel database. No videos are stored here.

Categories: news, sports, movies, series, entertainment, documentary, kids, animation, comedy, music, cooking, travel, science, education, business, weather, classic, auto. Every verified channel publishes a categories array, allowing membership in multiple categories. Duplicate streams and channel IDs are merged; favorites keep their IDs. Per-source scan counts distinguish metadata overlap from new stream candidates.

Daily validation samples each adaptive-live candidate twice, at least twelve seconds apart. Advancing HLS/dynamic DASH manifests qualify; finite clips and frozen manifests do not. Short rolling live windows are valid. Scan-time availability does not guarantee future host uptime, geographic access or media decoding. Source download failures or unusually empty scan results preserve the previous catalog. Clients download catalog.json daily or on Refresh. User-Agent/Referer options are retained.
